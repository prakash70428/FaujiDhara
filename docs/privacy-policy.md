# FaujiDhara – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 November 2026
**Applies to:** FaujiDhara Android app, version 1.0 and later 1.0.x updates
**Developer:** Prakash (individual developer)
**Contact:** [CONTACT_EMAIL]

## Summary

FaujiDhara version 1.0 **does not collect, store, or share any personal data**.
The app works fully offline. There is no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics.

## What the app does

FaujiDhara shows farming information: crop seasons, vegetables, fruits, flowers and spices for
each Indian state and union territory, and 3D models of plants with their real-world size.
All of this information is included inside the app.

## Data we collect

None. Specifically, version 1.0:

- does **not** ask for your name, phone number, email, or any account
- does **not** access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files
- does **not** use advertising IDs, analytics SDKs or crash reporting SDKs
- does **not** send any data from your device to our servers or to any third party

Choices you make in the app, such as the selected state or growth stage, stay in the app's memory
on your device while you use it and are not sent anywhere.

## Links to other websites

The "About" screen has links to this policy and to the app's public source code on GitHub.
If you open them, your browser loads those pages and their own privacy policies apply.

## Children

FaujiDhara is meant for farmers and veterans. It is not directed at children under 13
and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Future versions

Later versions may add optional features such as phone-number sign-in, a community feed and land
listings. Those features will need some personal data. Before any such version is released, we
will update this policy to explain exactly what is collected, why, how it is protected, and how
you can delete your account and data. The "Last updated" date above will change.
